# AI & LLM Fundamentals

---

## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
* **Definition:** Artificila intelligence is the aility of a computer or machine tp perform tasks taht normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning adn decision making.
* **Example:** When **Google Maps** suggests the fastest route to your destination by analyzing traffic conditions and road data in real time, it uses AI to make intelligent decisions and predicitions.
  
---

## 2. Generative AI
* **Definition:** Generative AI is a type of Artificial Intelligence that can create new content such as text, images, music, videos, or code based on the data it has learned from.
* **Example:** When you upload a photo and ask an AI tool to transform it into a Studio Ghibli-style artwork, the AI generates a new image in that artistic style. This is an example of Generative AI creating original visual content from your prompt or photo.
  
---

## 3. Large Language Model (LLM)
* **Definition:** A Large Language Model is a type of AI trained on vast amounts of text data to understand, generate an respond to human language.
*  **Example:** When you type "Write a leave application for school" and an AI instantly generates a complete, properly formatted application, it is using an LLM to understand your request and create a text.
  
---

## 4. Prompt Engineering
* **Definiton:** Prompt Engineering is the process of designing and refining instructions (prompts) given to an AI model to obtain accurate, relevant, and useful outputs.
*  **Example:** Instead of asking "Write a story", a prompt engineer might write "Write a 200 word mystery story set in a school, with three characters and a surprising ending." The detailed prompt helps the AI generate a more accurate an useful result.
  
---

## 5. How AI Applicaitons Work (The Workflow)
* **What it means:** In any modern app, using an AI feature is just a simple game of catch or a continous loop between the human user and the AI machine.

### The Interaction Chain
'User -> Prompt -> AI Model -> Response -> User'

* **Example:**
  1. **User:** You open Gemini on your phone.
  2. **Prompt:** You type: *"Write a 2-line sweet birthday text from my mom."*
  3. **AI Model:** The Gemini engine processes the words "birthday", "sweet", and "mom".
  4. **Response:** The engine generates: *"Happy Birthday to the most amazing mom! Thank you for always being my rock. Have a beautiful day!"*
  5. **User:** The text appears on your screen, and your copy-paste it into your messaging app to send to her.
