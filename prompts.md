##  2. Tournament Rules
* **My Prompt:** "Act as the Head Referee for Tournyx Esports. Write a professional, airtight, 4-point rules and regulations document for an upcoming tournament. It must leave zero loopholes. Cover: 1. Roster Restrictions (No smurfing/account sharing), 2. In-Game Glitches & Exploits, 3. Disconnection & Pause Policy, and 4. Admin Final Authority. Use an authoritative and clear tone."

### AI Output:
### 🏆 TOURNYX OFFICIAL COMPETITIVE RULEBOOK & CODES

**1. ROSTER INTEGRITY AND ACCOUNT ELIGIBILITY**
* **No Ringers/Smurfing:** All players must use their registered in-game names (IGN) and unique game IDs submitted during registration. Substituting unregistered players ("ringers") mid-tournament is strictly prohibited.
* **Account Sharing:** Players may only play on their own accounts. If an admin detects a sudden change in device, IP, or playstyle suggesting account sharing, the entire team will face immediate disqualification.

**2. EXPLOITS, GLITCHES, AND FAIR PLAY**
* **Banned Mechanics:** Utilizing any in-game bugs, unintended glitches, map exploits, or texture-peeking that gives an unfair tactical advantage will result in an immediate forfeit of that specific match.
* **Review Process:** If a team suspects an opponent used an exploit, they must submit video evidence to a Tournyx admin within 15 minutes of the match ending. Admin decisions on exploits are final.

**3. DISCONNECTION AND HARDWARE ISSUES**
* **Player Responsibility:** Players are fully responsible for their own internet connectivity, ping stability, and gaming hardware. 
* **Rematch Protocol:** Matches will not be paused or restarted for individual player disconnections once the countdown ends. A match will only be remade if the game server itself crashes or experiences major regional downtime affecting more than 50% of the lobby.

**4. ADMIN AUTHORITY AND FINAL DECISIONS**
* **Unforeseen Scenarios:** In any situation not explicitly covered by this rulebook, Tournyx tournament administrators reserve the right to make a judgment call on the spot to ensure fair play.
* **Final Ruling:** All decisions made by Tournyx Head Referees are absolute, final, and cannot be appealed or disputed by players or team managers.
